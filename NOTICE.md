### US Federal Plain Language Guidelines

The US Federal Plain Language Guidelines (plainlanguage.gov) are a work of the
United States government and are in the public domain. The rules in this skill
summarize and adapt that guidance with attribution. The guidelines are not a
product of this project, and this project is not affiliated with the US
Plain Language Action and Information Network (PLAIN) or with
plainlanguage.gov.

### ISO 24495-1:2023

ISO 24495-1:2023, Plain language, Part 1: Governing principles and elements,
is copyrighted by ISO. This project cites the standard by number and
paraphrases its governing principles. It does not reproduce the normative
text. The standard can be purchased from ISO. This skill is not an official
ISO product, is not approved or endorsed by ISO, and does not certify
compliance with ISO 24495-1. If a project must demonstrate compliance with
the standard, use the official text.

### Readability formulas

The Flesch Reading Ease and Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level formulas come from
published 1970s research and are freely implementable. Score outputs are
estimates, not certifications of clarity.
